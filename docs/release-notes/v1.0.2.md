First release of Zambodi Search, a Chrome extension that rebrands Google as Zambodi.

**Install:** download `zambodi-search-1.0.2.zip`, unzip it, open `chrome://extensions`,
turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped `zambodi-search` folder.

**What it does**
- Every visible "Google" on any page becomes "Zambodi", case preserved, including
  text inside iframes and open shadow roots.
- google.com and all 187 Google country domains show a 2000s-style bevelled Zambodi
  wordmark in place of the logo, with no flash of the original; the favicon and tab
  title follow.
- A 2004-style Zambodi new tab page, a "Zambodi" omnibox search engine (Windows and
  macOS), and a toolbar popup with one on/off switch.
- No network calls, no remote code, one stored flag.

**Known limits:** other Google products keep their own logos; the omnibox entry keeps
Google's favicon; closed shadow roots are not rewritten. See the README's Limitations.
